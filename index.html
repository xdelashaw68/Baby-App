On iPad

  • Mic and camera need the app opened from its own web address in Safari. Inside the Claude app they're blocked.
  • Lock him in: Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access. Triple-click the top button to start it, so swipes can't leave the app.
  • Music from other apps can't reach a web page. Use My songs (files in the Files app) or play from another device with Listen.
  • Tummy time: stand the iPad low in front of him. Faces and Mirror work well.

JeanLuc's Light Show

Slow, bold, high-contrast motion made for a two-month-old.

  • Dim the room and keep him 1–3 feet from the screen. The shapes are big on purpose.
  • Short and together. The screen rests itself after 5 minutes. Turning away, fussing, hiccups or yawns mean he's had enough.
  • Answer him. Tap the screen when he coos or smiles. Every touch makes a bloom right where the finger lands.
  • Controls: press and hold the round button in the corner. A quick baby swat won't open it.

Everything stays on this device. Camera and microphone are only used when you turn them on.

Rest time

Little eyes tire fast. Try again after a cuddle.
